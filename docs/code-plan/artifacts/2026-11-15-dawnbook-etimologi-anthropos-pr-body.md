## Summary

Adds the free Dawnbook book **Etimologi dan Derivasi Anthropos** by Iwan Kurniawan (13 content chapters plus Referensi, subject label Antropologi). Each chapter follows one derivative of the Greek *anthrōpos* (antropologi, filantropi, misantropi, antropomorfisme, antroposentrisme, antropoid, Antroposen, antropometri, antroponimi, antropofagi, likantropi) and the last chapter maps them together.
