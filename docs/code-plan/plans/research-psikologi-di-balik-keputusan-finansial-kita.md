## Bagian A: Teori Prospek, Loss Aversion, Disposition Effect (diverifikasi parent)

1. **Kahneman & Tversky (1979)**, Econometrica 47(2):263-291, doi:10.2307/1914185
   - PDF penuh dibaca (web.mit.edu). Abstrak: teori prospek sebagai kritik atas teori utilitas harapan; "people underweight outcomes that are merely probable in comparison with outcomes that are obtained with certainty".
   - **Certainty effect**: kecenderungan ini "contributes to risk aversion in choices involving sure gains and to risk seeking in choices involving sure losses".
   - **Reflection effect**: "the reflection of prospects around 0 reverses the preference order"; "risk aversion in the positive domain is accompanied by risk seeking in the negative domain".
   - Fungsi nilai: cembung ke bawah untuk kerugian dan "generally steeper for losses than for gains" (kerugian terasa lebih menyakitkan daripada kenikmatan keuntungan setara).
2. **Tversky & Kahneman (1992)**, "Advances in Prospect Theory", Journal of Risk and Uncertainty 5:297-323, doi:10.1007/BF00122574
   - Koefisien loss aversion lambda = 2,25. Diverifikasi via paper peer-reviewed Cambridge JDM ("Tversky and Kahneman's (1992) estimate of a loss aversion coefficient of 2.25..."). Catatan jujur: meta-analisis modern menunjukkan nilai sekitar 1,5 ("A value around or slightly below 1.50 seems more plausible"); boleh disebut sebagai catatan bila perlu.
3. **Shefrin & Statman (1985)**, Journal of Finance 40(3):777-790, doi:10.1111/j.1540-6261.1985.tb05002.x
   - Penamaan disposition effect; dikonfirmasi lewat teks Odean (1998): "The tendency to hold losers too long and sell winners too soon has been labeled the disposition effect by Shefrin and Statman (1985)".
4. **Odean (1998)**, "Are Investors Reluctant to Realize Their Losses?", Journal of Finance 53(5):1775-1798, doi:10.1111/0022-1082.00074
   - PDF penuh dibaca (faculty.haas.berkeley.edu). Data trading records 1987-1993 dari 10.000 akun discount brokerage.
   - "the ratio of PGR to PLR for the entire year is a little over 1.5, indicating that a stock that is up in value is more than 50 percent more likely to be sold" (rasio realisasi untung/rugi sekitar 1,5).
   - Investornya juga enggan meyakini kerugian; mengabaikan konsekuensi pajak (tax-loss selling yang optimal justru dilanggar).
5. **Benartzi & Thaler (1995)**, "Myopic Loss Aversion and the Equity Premium Puzzle", QJE 110(1):73-92, doi:10.2307/2118115
   - PDF NBER w4369 dibaca. Kombinasi loss aversion + evaluasi portofolio yang terlalu sering dinamai "myopic loss aversion".
   - Simulasi menunjukkan besarnya equity premium konsisten dengan investor yang mengevaluasi hasil investasinya setiap tahun (myopia).
6. **Kahneman & Tversky (1974)**, "Judgment under Uncertainty: Heuristics and Biases", Science 185(4157):1124-1131, doi:10.1126/science.185.4157.1124
   - PDF penuh dibaca (bear.warrington.ufl.edu). Eksperimen roda keberuntungan (wheel of fortune): "median estimates of African countries in the UN were 25 and 45 for groups that received 10 and 65, respectively, as starting points. Payoffs for accuracy did not reduce the anchoring effect."
   - Roda acak yang jelas acak saja memengaruhi estimasi; insentif akurasi tidak menghapus efek jangkar.

## Bagian B: Akuntansi Mental dan Rasa Sakit Membayar (diverifikasi subagent sa-research-B)

7. **Thaler (1999)**, "Mental Accounting Matters", Journal of Behavioral Decision Making 12(3):183-206, doi:10.1002/(SICI)1099-0771(199909)12:3<183::AID-BDM318>3.0.CO;2-F
   - PDF penuh dibaca (bear.warrington.ufl.edu). Tiga komponen mental accounting: (1) cara outcome dipersepsikan dan dievaluasi, (2) "assignment of activities to specific accounts", (3) frekuensi evaluasi akun ("choice bracketing", istilah Read, Loewenstein dan Rabin 1998).
   - "Each of the components of mental accounting violates the economic principle of fungibility."
   - Uang dilabeli tiga lapis: "expenditures are grouped into budgets (e.g. food, housing, etc.); wealth is allocated into accounts (e.g. checking, pension, 'rainy day'); and income is divided into categories (e.g. regular or windfall)".
   - Contoh sunk cost: pertandingan basket saat badai (Thaler 1980) di bab yang sama; "Perhaps the best decoupling device is the credit card."
   - DILARANG memakai contoh "kado pernikahan" (tidak ada di paper). Contoh resmi: pasangan pengolektor wine (hadiah mewah vs uang), donasi United Way, kontrak quilt, pertandingan basket badai.
8. **Tversky & Kahneman (1981)**, "The Framing of Decisions and the Psychology of Choice", Science 211(4481):453-458, doi:10.1126/science.7455683
   - PDF penuh dibaca (psych.hanover.edu). Masalah penyakit Asia: frame "menyelamatkan": Program A menyelamatkan 200 orang dipilih 72 persen; Program B (1/3 peluang menyelamatkan 600) 22 persen. Frame "meninggal": Program C (400 meninggal) 22 persen; Program D (2/3 peluang 600 meninggal) 78 persen.
   - "choices involving gains are often risk averse and choices involving losses are often risk taking. However, it is easy to see that the two problems are effectively identical."
   - Eksperimen tiket teater: kehilangan uang $10, 88 persen tetap membeli tiket; kehilangan tiket $10, hanya 46 persen membeli tiket kedua (tiket baru masuk ke akun yang sama sehingga pertunjukan terasa $20).
9. **Prelec & Loewenstein (1998)**, "The Red and the Black: Mental Accounting of Savings and Debt", Marketing Science 17(1):4-28, doi:10.1287/mksc.17.1.4
   - Abstrak resmi dibaca (EconPapers/JSTOR/Crossref; PDF CMU berupa scan tanpa teks). Teori akuntansi mental "double-entry" tentang interaksi dua arah "the pleasure of consumption and the pain of paying"; "The ticking of the taxi meter, for example, reduces one's pleasure from the ride."
   - "Some financing methods, such as credit cards, tend to weaken coupling, whereas others, such as cash payment, produce tight coupling."
   - Konsumsi yang sudah dibayar dapat dinikmati "as if it were free"; model memprediksi debt aversion dan preferensi prepay.
   - "Our model provides a better fit of the data for 60% of the subjects; the discounting formulation provides a better fit for only 29% of the subjects."
10. **Feinberg (1986)**, "Credit Cards as Spending Facilitating Stimuli: A Conditioning Interpretation", Journal of Consumer Research 13(3):348-356, **doi:10.1086/209074** (DOI 10.1086/208894 SALAH, itu milik Shimp & Bearden 1982)
    - Abstrak via JSTOR/PsycNet: "Four experiments and one study were conducted to test the hypothesis that stimuli associated with spending can elicit spending responses."; 135 pelanggan restoran + 154 mahasiswa.
    - "Credit card stimuli directed spending such that the probability, speed, or magnitude of spending was enhanced in the presence of credit card cues."
    - Angka efek (kutipan verbatim dari Prelec & Simester 2001, Marketing Letters 12:5-21, yang dibaca penuh): Feinberg "could boost hypothetical willingness-to-pay estimates by 50-200%"; waktu respons lebih cepat dengan stimulus kartu; donasi United Way rata-rata 11 sen (tunai) vs 33 sen (kartu).
    - Catatan replikasi (boleh dipakai sebagai kejujuran): Hunt, Chatterjee, Florsheim & Kernan (1990, Psychological Reports 67:323-330) tidak menemukan main effect.
11. **Raghubir & Srivastava (2008)**, "Monopoly Money: The Effect of Payment Coupling and Form on Spending Behavior", **Journal of Experimental Psychology: Applied** 14(3):213-225, doi:10.1037/1076-898X.14.3.213 (BUKAN JCR)
    - Abstrak APA PsycNet/ERIC: "Study 1 demonstrates that consumers are willing to spend more when a credit card logo is present versus absent."; "Study 3 demonstrates that consumers spend more when they are spending scrip (a form of stored value certificate) versus cash of the same face value."
    - Artikel penulis di NYU SternBusiness (dibaca penuh): kartu hadiah "serves to anaesthetize the pain of paying"; mekanisme transparansi pembayaran: "the more transparent the payment outflow, the greater the aversion to spending."

## Bagian C: Bias Masa Kini, Discounting, dan Rasionalitas Terbatas (diverifikasi subagent sa-research-C)

12. **Simon (1955)**, "A Behavioral Model of Rational Choice", QJE 69(1):99-118, doi:10.2307/1884852
    - PDF arsip CMU dibaca. Klaim inti: mengganti "the global rationality of economic man" dengan rasionalitas yang sesuai akses informasi dan kapasitas komputasi organisme nyata.
    - Payoff direduksi jadi (satisfactory, unsatisfactory); "aspiration level"; "The player, instead of seeking for a 'best' move, needs only to look for a 'good' move."
    - PERINGATAN istilah: kata "satisficing" DAN "bounded rationality" TIDAK muncul di teks 1955. Tulis konsepnya, jangan atribusikan istilahnya ke paper ini.
13. **Laibson (1997)**, "Golden Eggs and Hyperbolic Discounting", QJE 112(2):443-478 (Crossref; DASH menulis 443-477), **doi:10.1162/003355397555253** (DOI 10.2307/2951242 hanyalah ID JSTOR)
    - PDF Harvard DASH dibaca. Discount quasi-hyperbolic: "the discount structure in equation (1) mimics the qualitative property of the hyperbolic discount function"; nilai {1, bd, bd^2, ...} (b=beta, d=delta); contoh parameter b=0,6 dan d=0,99.
    - "The preferences given by equation (1) are dynamically inconsistent, in the sense that preferences at date t are inconsistent with preferences at date t + 1."
    - Metafora golden eggs: aset likuid rendah menjanjikan manfaat besar jangka panjang tapi sulit dicairkan segera; persis telur emas angsa.
    - Istilah "present-biased preferences" TIDAK ada di paper 1997 ini (dipakai di O'Donoghue & Rabin 1999).
14. **O'Donoghue & Rabin (1999)**, "Doing It Now or Later", American Economic Review 89(1):103-124, doi:10.1257/aer.89.1.103
    - PDF Cornell dibaca. Mencetuskan istilah: "We have contrived the term 'present-biased preferences'..."; "present-biased preferences give stronger relative weight to the earlier moment as it gets closer."
    - "Naive people procrastinate immediate-cost activities and preproperate—do too soon—immediate-reward activities."
    - Sophisticates vs naifs: "Sophistication mitigates procrastination, but exacerbates preproperation."
15. **McClure, Laibson, Loewenstein & Cohen (2004)**, Science 306(5695):503-507, doi:10.1126/science.1100907
    - PDF dibaca (worthylab.org). n = 14 subjek.
    - "Parts of the limbic system associated with the midbrain dopamine system, including paralimbic cortex, are preferentially activated by decisions involving immediately available rewards. In contrast, regions of the lateral prefrontal cortex and posterior parietal cortex are engaged uniformly by intertemporal choices irrespective of delay."
    - Area beta (immediate): ventral striatum, medial orbitofrontal cortex, medial prefrontal cortex. Aktivitas relatif area beta vs delta berkorelasi dengan pilihan; interaksi signifikan (P < 0,005).
16. **Ashraf, Karlan & Yin (2006)**, "Tying Odysseus to the Mast: Evidence From a Commitment Savings Product in the Philippines", QJE 121(2):635-672, doi:10.1162/qjec.121.2.635
    - PDF penuh dibaca (navaashraf.com). Produk tabungan komitmen SEED ("Save, Earn, Enjoy Deposits") di bank Filipina, RCT.
    - "After twelve months, average savings balances increased by 81 percentage points for those clients assigned to the treatment group relative to control" (ITT); kelompok komitmen menabung 47-82 persen lebih banyak tergantung spesifikasi (Table VI).
    - Orang yang teridentifikasi present-biased lebih tertarik membuka akun komitmen.

## Bagian D: Diverifikasi parent langsung (untuk bab default/status quo dan overconfidence)

17. **Madrian & Shea (2001)**, "The Power of Suggestion: Inertia in 401(k) Participation and Savings Behavior", QJE 116(4):1149-1187, doi:10.1162/003355301753265543
    - PDF NBER w7682 dibaca penuh. Pendaftaran otomatis 401(k): "approximately 86% of employees hired under automatic enrollment participate in the 401(k) plan", versus 49% kohort lama dengan masa kerja serupa (3-5 bulan) di bawah rezim opt-in lama (Figure 3: NEW 86% vs OLD 49%).
    - Default bukan hanya menaikkan partisipasi: mayoritas peserta baru juga terjebak di default kontribusi dan alokasi dana; 61% peserta auto-enrollment tidak melakukan perubahan apa pun.
18. **Thaler & Benartzi (2004)**, "Save More Tomorrow: Using Behavioral Economics to Increase Employee Saving", Journal of Political Economy 112(1):S164-S187, doi:10.1086/380085
    - Program SMarT: komitmen sekarang untuk menyisihkan porsi kenaikan gaji di masa depan. Tingkat tabungan peserta naik dari 3,5 persen menjadi 13,6 persen selama 40 bulan (diverifikasi via abstrak penerbit + DOL CLEAR + behavioraleconomicslab).
19. **Barber & Odean (2000)**, "Trading Is Hazardous to Your Wealth: The Common Stock Investment Performance of Individual Investors", Journal of Finance 55(2):773-806, doi:10.1111/0022-1082.00226
    - PDF penuh dibaca (faculty.haas.berkeley.edu). Dari 66.465 rumah tangga di satu discount brokerage 1991-1996: "those that trade most earn an annual return of 11.4 percent, while the market returns 17.9 percent"; yang jarang berdagang memperoleh 18,5 persen.

## Bagian E: Anchoring, Overconfidence, Herding, Status Quo, dan Pertahanan Diri (diverifikasi subagent sa-research-D)

20. **Kahneman (2011)**, Thinking, Fast and Slow, Farrar, Straus and Giroux (c) 2011
    - Verbatim dari buku: "System 1 operates automatically and quickly, with little or no effort and no sense of voluntary control."; "System 2 allocates attention to the effortful mental activities that demand it, including complex computations."
    - Soal kelelawar-dan-bola: "A bat and ball cost $1.10. The bat costs one dollar more than the ball. How much does the ball cost?"; jawaban benar 5 sen; "The distinctive mark of this easy puzzle is that it evokes an answer that is intuitive, appealing, and wrong."
21. **Ariely, Loewenstein & Prelec (2003)**, "Coherent Arbitrariness: Stable Demand Curves Without Stable Preferences", QJE 118(1):73-106 (Crossref; kutipan umum 73-98), **doi:10.1162/00335530360535153** (DOI 10.1162/00335530360535136 TIDAK valid)
    - Leksol eksperimen dengan 55 mahasiswa MBA MIT Sloan, prosedur insentif BDM, 6 produk (aksesori komputer, wine, cokelat, buku; TIDAK ada mousepad).
    - "Subjects were asked whether they would buy each good for a dollar figure equal to the last two digits of their social security number."
    - "Subjects with above-median social security numbers stated values from 57 percent to 107 percent greater than did subjects with below-median numbers."
    - Keyboard tanpa kabel: subjek kuintil atas rata-rata bersedia membayar $56, kuintil bawah $16. "the impact of the social security number on stated WTP was significant in every product category."
22. **Stewart (2009)**, "The Cost of Anchoring on Credit-Card Minimum Repayments", Psychological Science 20(1):39-41, doi:10.1111/j.1467-9280.2008.02255.x
    - ARAH TEMUAN PENTING: jangkar minimum payment justru MENURUNKAN besaran pembayaran.
    - Survei (n=75, pembayaran parsial): korelasi positif signifikan antara minimum repayment dan pembayaran aktual (Spearman r = 0,57, p < 0,0001).
    - Eksperimen (tagihan tiruan £435,76, minimum £5,42): "Removing minimum-repayment information had a dramatic effect"; rata-rata pembayaran naik 70%, dari £99 (23% saldo) menjadi £175 (40% saldo), Wilcoxon p < 0,0001.
    - Info minimum payment tidak mengubah probabilitas lunas penuh (54,8% vs 55,1%, ns); "a 2% reduction in minimum repayments roughly quadruples interest charges".
23. **Barber & Odean (2001)**, "Boys Will Be Boys: Gender, Overconfidence, and Common Stock Investment", QJE 116(1):261-292, doi:10.1162/003355301556400
    - Data >35.000 rumah tangga discount brokerage, Februari 1991 s.d. Januari 1997.
    - "We document that men trade 45 percent more than women."
    - "Trading reduces men's net returns by 2.65 percentage points a year as opposed to 1.72 percentage points for women."
24. **Bikhchandani, Hirshleifer & Welch (1992)**, "A Theory of Fads, Fashion, Custom, and Cultural Change as Informational Cascades", JPE 100(5):992-1026, doi:10.1086/261849
    - Definisi: "An informational cascade occurs when it is optimal for an individual, having observed the actions of those ahead of him, to follow the behavior of the preceding individual without regard to his own information."
    - "localized conformity of behavior and the fragility of mass behaviors can be explained by informational cascades"
    - "Informational cascades explain why society, on the basis of little information, will systematically tend to land close to the borderline, causing fragility."; "at some stage a decision maker will ignore his private information and act only on the information obtained from previous decisions."
25. **Samuelson & Zeckhauser (1988)**, "Status Quo Bias in Decision Making", Journal of Risk and Uncertainty 1(1):7-59 (BUKAN 39-52), doi:10.1007/BF00055564
    - "A series of decision-making experiments shows that individuals disproportionately stick with the status quo."
    - "for the large majority of alternatives, the percentage response rate is highest when the alternative is in the SQ position, lower in the NEUT position, and lowest in the ASQ position."
    - Data riil alokasi dana pensiun TIAA/CREF (850.000 peserta): "only 28% of those surveyed had ever changed their distribution of premium between the funds"; "fewer than 2.5% of all participants alter their distribution in a given year."
    - Rencana kesehatan Harvard: "only 3% of Harvard employees switched plans each year."
26. **Thaler & Sunstein (2008)**, Nudge: Improving Decisions About Health, Wealth, and Happiness, Yale University Press, ISBN 978-0-300-12223-7
    - "if, for a given choice, there is a default option, an option that will prevail if the chooser does nothing, then we can usually expect a large number of people to end up with that option"; "Defaults are ubiquitous and powerful."
    - "The Save More Tomorrow plan invites participants to commit themselves, in advance, to a series of contribution increases timed to coincide with pay raises."
27. **Benartzi & Thaler (2013)**, "Behavioral Economics and the Retirement Savings Crisis", Science 339(6124):1152-1153, doi:10.1126/science.1231320
    - Skala adopsi automatic escalation: "we estimate that there are already about 4.1 million participants who are having their savings rates automatically increased"; "We estimate that automatic escalation boosted annual savings by $7.4 billion"
    - Dari penawaran SMarT asli: 78% karyawan mendaftar ("Of the employees offered the original version of SMT, 78% signed up").
    - ANGKA DILARANG: "15 juta orang" hanya klaim promosi situs Benartzi; "$20 billion" TIDAK terverifikasi di sumber primer mana pun.
    - Catatan bibliografi: paper SMarT asli terbit di JPE 112(S1):S164-S187 (suplemen).

## Catatan interpretasi (untuk konsistensi antar bab)

- Lambda 2,25 selalu disebut "estimasi Tversky dan Kahneman (1992)"; jika menyebut meta-analisis modern, sebut sekitar 1,5. Jangan campur dalam satu kalimat angka tunggal.
- Madrian & Shea: tulis "dari sekitar 49% menjadi 86% untuk pekerja baru dengan masa kerja serupa", bukan "partisipasi semua karyawan naik".
- SMarT: "3,5% menjadi 13,6% dalam 40 bulan", selalu sebut keduanya dan rentang waktunya.
- Barber & Odean 2000: 11,4% adalah return kelompok yang berdagang paling aktif, dibanding pasar 17,9% dan kelompok paling jarang berdagang 18,5%; selalu sebut "net annualized geometric mean".
- Prelec & Loewenstein: pain of paying adalah interaksi dua arah (konsumsi menurunkan rasa sakit bayar, bayar menurunkan kenikmatan); jangan sederhanakan jadi "kartu = jahat".
- Feinberg: kutip angka 50-200% sebagai "estimasi kenaikan kesediaan membayar hipotetis" dari deskripsi Prelec & Simester 2001 atas Feinberg 1986, dan sebut kejujuran bahwa replikasi 1990 tidak menemukan efek utama.
- T&K 1981: 72% vs 78% adalah pilihan pada masalah yang secara matematis identik (frame nyawa vs kematian); tiket teater 88% vs 46%.
- Mental accounting: istilah kunci fungibility (uang bisa dipertukarkan) dilanggar oleh mental accounting.
- SEED: 81 poin persentase adalah kenaikan saldo tabungan (ITT), 47-82% adalah spesifikasi efek; jangan tulis "menabung 81% lebih banyak" tanpa konteks.
- Stewart 2009: arah temuan WAJIB benar; adanya info minimum payment menurunkan rata-rata pembayaran (menghapus info menaikkan rata-rata pembayaran 70%, £99 menjadi £175). Jangan menulis "minimum payment membuat orang membayar lebih banyak".
- Ariely dkk. 2003: produknya keyboard/trackball/wine/cokelat/buku (tidak ada mousepad); angka utama: nilai di atas-median SSN 57-107% lebih tinggi; keyboard $56 vs $16.
- Samuelson & Zeckhauser: kutip data riil (28% pernah mengubah alokasi TIAA/CREF; <2,5% per tahun; 3% pindah rencana kesehatan Harvard), bukan angka eksperimen yang tidak diverifikasi.
- SMarT follow-up: pakai 4,1 juta peserta dan $7,4 miliar (Benartzi & Thaler 2013, Science); DILARANG memakai 15 juta atau $20 miliar.
- Semua angka persen dalam teks Indonesia ditulis dengan koma desimal (0,66; 2,25; 17,9%) dan kata "persen" atau tanda %.
