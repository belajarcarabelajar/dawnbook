## From section 29 (Cooked Prose, Not Raw Sources (Owner Rule, 2026-10-24))

- **Why this exists:** the book "Pelajaran Hidup dari Harvest Moon Back to Nature" (PR #184) went live with every fact correct and every gate green, and the owner found it stiff, AI-like and not pleasant to read ("gak enak dibaca", "tulisan yang tidak memiliki jiwa"). The sources were served raw, like sashimi, instead of cooked. A reader who meets hollow prose leaves, and a book without a voice damages the whole platform. The owner calls this failure fatal; correctness alone is not done.
- **Measured symptoms (13 chapters):** "Panduan ..." as the subject of a sentence 50 times, "tokoh kita" 51, "Menurut panduan" 27, "panduan resmi Prima" 24, "mirip dengan" 16, "Kalau dipikir-pikir" 14, and the same shape in every chapter (scene, source facts, one study with stacked caveats, 4 to 5 recap bullets).
- **Mechanical guard:** a book test should cap the measured scaffolds per chapter. Example: `tests/books/pelajaran-hidup-dari-harvest-moon-back-to-nature.test.ts` caps 0 "Kalau dipikir-pikir", 0 "Menurut panduan", 0 stock connectors, 0 "bab berikutnya", at most 2 "tokoh kita" and at most 2 mentions of the word "panduan" per chapter. A green guard is necessary, not sufficient: the parent audit and the reviewers judge the voice too.
- **Build gate (added 2026-10-24):** `scripts/check-voice.ts` holds the shared caps (0 "Kalau dipikir-pikir", "Menurut panduan", "tulis panduan", stock connectors "Perhatikan(lah)", "Di situlah letak(nya)", "Menariknya", "Ada satu detail/catatan", and "bab berikutnya"; at most 1 "mirip dengan" and 2 "tokoh kita" per chapter). `bun run build` runs it over every book before the mdBook builds and exits 1 on any hit (`HEAVY_LOCAL=check-voice bun run check:voice` runs the same check alone). A line that quotes a source verbatim, such as a classroom transcript, may keep a flagged word when it ends with `<!-- voice-ok: <reason> -->`; the reason is mandatory and the marker is for verbatim quotes only, never for the author's own prose. The 2026-10-24 scaffold pass (plan 2026-10-24-dawnbook-voice-scaffold-48-books) brought all 60 books to 0. Since 2026-11-02 it also enforces item 9 in every checked book (the free books after their four-wave sweep, plan 2026-11-02-free-books-contrast-sweep, and the premium books with `"edition": 2026`), at 0 per chapter for "bukan ... tetapi/tapi/melainkan/namun" within one sentence and for the trailing ", bukan", headings included; it does not count blockquote lines, fenced code blocks (Mermaid diagrams), or the tag question ", bukan?". Reveal colons are not checked mechanically; reviewers catch them. Since 2026-11-02 it also enforces item 10 in every checked book, at 0 per chapter for "Kekuatan bukti:" (any bold form) and for a "Rujukan:" tag right before a link.
